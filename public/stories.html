Gitanjali Rao: The Teen Who Detected Lead in Water

At 11 years old, she invented Tethys — a device to detect lead in drinking water — after learning about the Flint Water Crisis. She then built Kindly, an AI app to detect cyberbullying. TIME named her their first-ever Kid of the Year. Today she studies at MIT.
